How it works

No new equipment. No new app.

  1. 01Send a photo of the day's page

    Your manager sends a photo of the notebook page with the opening dip, deliveries, pump meter readings and closing dip, the records you already keep.

  2. 02We check the numbers

    We read the page, confirm the readings with the sender, then compare the fuel that left each tank with what the pumps say was sold.

  3. 03You get the result on WhatsApp

    A short message every evening, with a clear warning when fuel is missing or a delivery wasn't recorded, and a weekly summary with the value in shillings.

expected closing = opening + delivered − sold

Every evening, the fuel that should be in the tank is compared with what's actually there.

Small differences are normal, because fuel expands and shrinks with heat and dipsticks aren't perfect. Those are ignored.

A bigger gap gets flagged the same evening, with the litres and the value in shillings, so you can check the shift while it's fresh, not weeks later.
